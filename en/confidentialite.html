Personal data

In short

Pom'chess works without an account: in that case, no personal data is sent and your settings stay on your device.

With an account (optional), we only keep the account holder's first name, surname and e-mail address, and for a pupil account each child's first name, surname, class and progress. We never share them with any third party, we never sell them, and there are no ads and no trackers.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Mathieu Pouillon, creator of Pom'chess, acting in a personal, non-commercial capacity.

For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights if you can't do it from your account: write to the contact address given here.

The data we keep

  • The account holder: first name, surname, e-mail address, account type (player, pupil, teacher) and creation date.
  • The children of a pupil account: first name, surname, class joined, creation date and date of last activity.
  • A teacher's classes: their name and code.
  • Progress: for each game, experience (XP), the number of successes and games played, the best challenge result.
  • Game settings (theme, board colour, chosen level…), so you find them on another device.

No date of birth, no photo, no location, no sensitive data. Voice dictation in "Blindfold" uses your device's or browser's speech recognition: Pom'chess keeps no recording of your voice.

Why, and on what basis

This data is only used to run your account: recognise you when you sign in, keep your settings and progress, and let a teacher open games for their class and follow their pupils.

The legal basis is your consent, given when you create the account, and the provision of the service you ask for (Article 6 of the GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account.

For children, a parent (or legal guardian) creates the pupil account with their own e-mail address and adds their children: they thereby give the consent required for minors.

Who sees what

  • The account holder sees all their data, and their children's.
  • A class's teacher sees, for each pupil in their class: first name, surname, profile creation date, last activity and progress. They do not see the parent's e-mail address.
  • Nobody else. These rules are enforced by the server itself, not just by the website.

Technical providers

To run accounts, Pom'chess uses technical providers, who process the data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: the database and sign-in, with servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Supabase is bound by a GDPR-compliant data processing agreement.
  • If you choose to sign in with Google, Facebook (Meta) or LinkedIn: that service knows you signed in to Pom'chess, and passes us your name and e-mail address. Signing in by e-mail avoids this.
  • The website is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads its font from Google Fonts: as for any website, they receive your device's IP address. The Android app does not use Google Fonts.

How long

Data is kept as long as the account exists. When you delete your account, everything is erased immediately: the account, the children's profiles, the classes and the progress. Accounts with no sign-in for three years may be deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, erase it, get it in a file (portability), object to its processing or ask for its restriction.

From the My account page, you can do it yourself: correct your first name and surname, download all your data, delete a child's profile or delete your account.

You can also lodge a complaint with your country's data protection authority: in France, the CNIL (cnil.fr); in Belgium, the Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be); in the UK, the ICO (ico.org.uk).

On your device

Pom'chess keeps your settings and, if you are signed in, your session in the local storage of your browser or the app. There are no advertising cookies and no audience measurement.

Exchanges with the server are encrypted (HTTPS).
